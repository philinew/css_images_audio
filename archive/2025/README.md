# 2025 edition

Computational Social Science with Images and Audio, ETH Zurich, Fall Term 2025, Course No. 851-0762-00L

**Lecturers:** Elliott Ash & Philine Widmer

The course comprised 5 sessions of 4 lessons each (Fridays, 12:15–16:00).

What the slides cover:

- **1a**: images as social science data, what an image is, classical techniques, CNNs, and vision transformers
- **1b**: research examples on facial recognition AI, data, and the state
- **2**: classical machine learning with explicit feature extraction (color histograms, texture and entropy, HOG, Haar-like features)
- **4**: CNNs in more detail, generating images with GANs, and audio analysis (sound basics, spectrograms, MFCCs, deep learning for audio)

## Exam

The exam took place on 12 December 2025. It was a closed-book multiple-choice exam with 41 questions and lasted 60 minutes. It covered the slides, the tutorials, the research input, and the student presentations.

- [Exam](exam/exam_2025.pdf)
- [Mock exam](exam/mock_exam_2025.pdf)

The group presentation counted for 30% of the final grade and the exam for 70%.
