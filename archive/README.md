# Archive: past editions

This folder contains the material from past editions of "Computational Social Science with Images and Audio" at ETH Zurich (course no. 851-0762-00L).

| Edition | Lecturers | Format | Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| [2025](2025/) | Elliott Ash, Philine Widmer | 5 Friday blocks of 4 lessons | slides, tutorials, research input, student presentations, mock exam, exam |
| [2023](2023/) | Elliott Ash, Philine Widmer | weekly sessions | slides, quizzes, tutorials, research input, student presentations, exam |

Each edition has the same folder structure:

- `slides/`: lecture slides
- `tutorials/`: tutorials (Jupyter notebooks, written for Google Colab)
- `research_input/`: slides from the research input sessions
- `student_presentations/`: slides of the student paper presentations
- `exam/`: the exam (and, where available, the mock exam)

The content of the course changes from year to year, and so do the papers that students present. If you are taking the class this year, the [current syllabus](../README.md) is what counts. Use the archive as additional reading and as inspiration.
